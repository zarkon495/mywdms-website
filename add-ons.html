Add-Ons

Extend myWDMS with the Add-Ons You Need

The core platform covers account access, billing, rates, and reporting out of the box. These optional add-ons go further, so you only pay for what your operation actually uses.

Every add-on below plugs directly into your myWDMS account. Start with the core platform and add what you need, when you need it, no separate systems to manage.

🏦

Lockbox

Automated processing of mailed-in check payments. Checks are captured, deposited, and reconciled directly to the right customer account, no manual data entry required.

Add-On
📧

eBilling

Paperless invoicing and statements delivered straight to your customers' email. Faster delivery, lower printing costs, and a full digital record of every bill sent.

Add-On
⚡

Quick Pay

A guest checkout link customers can use to pay a bill in seconds, no account or login required. Great for one-time payments and fewer calls to your billing team.

Add-On
💬

Pay by Text

Send payment reminders and secure pay links straight to your customers' phones. Customers can view their balance and pay in a few taps.

Add-On
📞

IVR Payments

An automated phone payment line so customers can pay their balance anytime, day or night, without tying up your staff.

Add-On
📅

Payment Plans

Let customers split a balance into scheduled installments instead of one lump sum, automatically tracked and billed through the platform.

Add-On
✉️

Print & Mail

We print and mail physical invoices, statements, and written notices to your customers on your behalf, so your team never has to run to the post office.

Add-On
🗺️

Routing

Assign, optimize, and track service routes for every truck in your fleet, with real-time visibility into stops, drivers, and progress.

Add-On
