About Us

Who We Are

myWDMS is headquartered in Irvine, California and was built to provide comprehensive waste management software to haulers, municipalities, and roll-off operators throughout the United States.

Our team brings more than 30 years of direct experience in the waste and hauling industry. We have spent that time working alongside commercial haulers, residential service providers, roll-off operators, and public works departments. myWDMS was built because we understood exactly what this industry needed and what existing software repeatedly failed to provide.

That depth of experience shows in how the platform is designed. Every feature, from route management to lockbox billing to compliance reporting, was shaped by people who have seen firsthand where waste operations break down. The result is software that fits the way your team actually works, not the other way around.

The myWDMS platform is powerful and built specifically for waste operations, while offering the flexibility to adapt to your unique workflows. With a team focused on seamless integration and responsive customer service, myWDMS brings together route management, billing, accounts receivable, banking, finance, and accounting in one unified system.

We build real partnerships with every customer we work with, from municipalities to commercial haulers to roll-off operators. Because our team has spent decades in this industry, we understand the challenges your dispatchers, billing staff, and drivers face every day. That experience shapes everything we build and every conversation we have.
